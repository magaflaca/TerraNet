## Full description (max 4000)

TerraNet turns your phone into a server for playing Terraria with your friends. Create or import a world, tap Host and you get a public address to share. Your friends join from their own Terraria, wherever they are, without touching the router or forwarding ports.

How it works
- The server runs on your own device, powered by TShock.
- TerraNet gives it a public address and port on play.hacks.win, reserved for your device.
- It keeps running in the background while your friends play, with a notification to stop it whenever you want.

What you can do
- Create worlds with size, difficulty, evil and seed, or import one you already have.
- Rename and export your worlds.
- Share the world you already opened in Terraria without starting another server.
- Console bridge: shows an internet server in the local games of the consoles at home and relays the connection.
- Players on Terraria 1.4.5 and 1.4.6 can join.
- Plays like vanilla by default. Every TShock option is one tap away when you want more: PvP, spawn protection, anti-cheat, accounts, UUID login, server-side characters and world rules.
- Add your own TShock plugins.
- Welcome message with Terraria colors.
- Export your server setup to a .zip to keep going on a regular TShock.
- Start with the device and reopen the last world.
- English and Spanish, light and dark themes, all in pixel art.

Requirements
- You need your own copy of Terraria to play.
- Generating large worlds needs a powerful phone (8 GB of RAM or more). On modest devices you can import a ready world or share the one you opened in Terraria.
